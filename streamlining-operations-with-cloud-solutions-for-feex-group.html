About The Project

Team Oreo worked with FEEX Group, a mid-sized Chicago manufacturer, to modernize its outdated SaaS. Goals were better operational efficiency, lower costs, and improved scalability. The team delivered a tailored cloud-based solution for seamless data access and collaboration across locations. The six-month project covered planning and data migration.

Problems

FEEX relied on legacy systems that caused frequent downtime, data silos, and high maintenance costs. Its on-premise servers struggled with growing workloads, causing productivity losses. Remote staff had trouble accessing critical systems. The company needed a scalable solution that also ensured data security and industry compliance.

Challenges

Employees resisted changing from familiar systems. Data migration risked corruption or loss and needed careful planning. Downtime had to be minimized during the transition. Integrating the new cloud system with existing applications required custom configuration. Cybersecurity and manufacturing compliance standards also had to be addressed.

Our Solutions

The team deployed a hybrid cloud combining private and public clouds. A phased migration reduced downtime, and automated backups protected data integrity. Custom APIs connected legacy software to the new system. Training sessions and post-implementation support aided adoption. Encryption and multi-factor authentication strengthened security.

Result

  • Reduced operational costs by 30%
  • Improved system reliability
