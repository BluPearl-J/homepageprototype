Nonprofit · Illustrative example

Case study 01

Making digital donations simpler and more transparent

Problem

A nonprofit needs a reliable way to accept donations while reducing administration.

Analysis

Map donor journeys, payment handling, reporting needs and safeguarding requirements.

Solution

A clear donation experience with automated acknowledgement and internal reporting.

Technology

Responsive web app, secure payment integration and analytics dashboard.

Expected outcome

A simpler donor journey and less manual coordination. Outcomes to be validated.

Hospitality · Hypothetical case study

Case study 02

Bringing restaurant ordering into one connected flow

Problem

Phone and in-person orders create errors, delays and limited customer insight.

Analysis

Review ordering peaks, menu management, kitchen handoffs and fulfilment.

Solution

An accessible ordering journey connected to the restaurant’s operating workflow.

Technology

Web ordering, menu CMS, payments and live operational reporting.

Expected outcome

Fewer handoff points and clearer demand insight. Outcomes to be validated.
