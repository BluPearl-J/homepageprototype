About The Project

Team Oreo built Locate Me, a mobile app for reporting emergencies such as domestic violence, sexual assault, or public harassment. It shares real-time location and live video with trusted contacts and police, and offers shake and voice triggers for discreet use. Additional features include a mental health chatbot and a “report a crime” function. The app has been delivered to relevant organisations.

Problems

Traditional emergency systems are slow, inflexible, and not discreet, which can increase danger for people reporting abuse. Victims often hesitate to report because of fear, stigma, or lack of timely support. Government responses have focused on policing and overlooked tech-enabled support. Reports of abuse rose during the pandemic, exposing gaps in prevention and response.

Challenges

The main challenge was balancing functionality with confidentiality, including sending alerts without the attacker knowing. Other challenges were preventing false alarms without reducing responsiveness, meeting privacy and data protection requirements, and building a reliable cross-platform system with minimal data and battery use. Coordinating with law enforcement to create an actionable response was also critical.

Our Solutions

Locate Me combines location tracking, live streaming, and AI chatbot support. Users can send silent alerts by shake or button, with a pop-up confirmation to prevent accidental alerts. Police receive real-time location and video, and users can limit sharing to law enforcement. The “ask for help” feature can alert nearby verified volunteers. AI mental health checks flag distress and connect users to professionals.
